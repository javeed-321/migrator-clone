# Introduction

Capillary is an AI-powered, cloud-native SaaS platform for loyalty management, omnichannel marketing, and customer intelligence. This guide walks you through your first steps on the platform, from logging in to launching your first program, campaign, or report and links you to the deeper documentation for each area.

## What Capillary offers

All Capillary products run inside a single interface called **InTouch**, with one shared navigation panel. Depending on what your organization has licensed, you'll have access to some or all of the following:

| Product                           | What it does                                                                                          | Learn more                                                                                                            |
| :-------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Loyalty+**                      | Build omnichannel loyalty programs that reward customers for desired behavior and drive repeat sales. | <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/loyalty-overview">Loyalty+ Overview</Anchor>        |
| **Engage+**                       | Personalize customer engagement across channels with segmentation, campaigns, and journeys.           | <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/engage-overview">Engage+ Overview</Anchor>          |
| **Insights+**                     | Get AI-powered customer analytics, reports, and dashboards to act on.                                 | <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/insights-plus-overview">Insights+ Overview</Anchor> |
| **Capillary Data Platform (CDP)** | Ingest, unify, and manage customer data from any source.                                              | [Data Entities](https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/data-entities)                                                    |
| **Rewards+**                      | Manage the catalog of rewards customers can redeem across programs.                                   | [Rewards+ Overview](https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/rewards_overview)                                             |
| **aiRA**                          | Capillary's AI assistant for faster, guided workflows across the platform.                            | [Getting Started with aiRA](https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/aira-getting-started)                                 |

> Note: Product availability depends on your organization's license. If you don't see a module mentioned here in your InTouch navigation panel, contact your Capillary account team.

## Key concepts

A few terms come up across every Capillary product:

* Organization (org): Your brand's account on Capillary, the container for all your users, data, and configuration.
* InTouch: The shared web application that hosts every Capillary product module.
* Members / Customers: The end consumers your brand engages with, enrolled in loyalty programs, targeted by campaigns, and analyzed in reports.
* Access groups and roles: Control which modules and actions each InTouch user can see and perform. See [Identity and Access Management](https://docs.capillarytech.com/docs/overview-iam).
