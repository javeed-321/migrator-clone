---
title: "Getting Started with Capillary"
---

Capillary is an AI-powered platform for loyalty management, omnichannel engagement, and customer analytics, all accessed through one shared interface, InTouch. This guide gets you logged in, oriented, and through your first task in each product you've licensed.

For a conceptual look at how Capillary's products, entities, and data fit together, see the [Capillary Overview](https://docs.capillarytech.com/update/docs/introduction).

---

## Step 1: Access Capillary

Before you do anything else, you need your organization's cluster URL and login credentials.

1. Get your cluster URL and credentials from your Capillary onboarding contact.
2. Sign in using your Capillary credentials, your Capillary Google Workspace account, or your organization's SSO — whichever your org has set up.

Full details on cluster URLs, login methods, and setting up SSO or MFA are in [Accessing Capillary](/accessing-capillary).

## Step 2: Get oriented in InTouch

Once you're signed in, spend a few minutes exploring the navigation panel — it's how you'll reach every product and module.

See [Explore InTouch Navigation](/explore-intouch-navigation) for a tour of the Home, aiRA, Loyalty, Engage, Rewards, Audiences, Channels, Member Care, Insights, Data Management, Extensions, and Settings sections.

## Step 3: Set up your team's access

Before rolling out to your wider team, decide who needs access to what.

1. Review [User management](/new-user-management) to understand how to manage users.
2. Add your team members and assign access using [Onboarding and Managing Users](/onboarding-user).

> Note: Only Organisation owners can assign access. If you're not sure who that is for your org, check with your Capillary account team.

---

## Step 4: Get started with each product

Once you're in and your team has access, here's where to start in each product you've licensed.

### Loyalty+ — build your first loyalty program

1. Read the [Loyalty+ Overview](/loyalty-overview) to understand programs, tiers, and points.
2. Explore the [Building Blocks of Loyalty](/building-blocks-of-loyalty) — points, tiers, and rules that make up a program.
3. Set up rewards for members to redeem in the [Rewards catalogue](/incentives-rewards-catalog).
4. If you need more than one program (e.g., regional or brand-specific variants), see [Multi-Loyalty Programs](/create-a-multi-loyalty-program).

### Engage+ — launch your first campaign

1. Define who you want to reach — see [Create Audience Group](/create-audience-group).
2. Build and send your first campaign — see [Create Campaign](/create-campaign).
3. For always-on, trigger-based engagement instead of a one-off send, explore [Journey Management](/journey-management).

### Insights+ — get your first report

1. Read the [Insights+ Overview](/insights-plus-overview) to see what's available out of the box.
2. Create your first custom report — see [Create and Publish Custom Report](/create).

### Capillary Data Platform — connect your data

1. Review [Data Entities](/data-entities) to understand how Capillary models customer and transaction data.
2. Connect a data source — see [Connect+ Getting Started](/connectplus-getting-started).

### aiRA — work faster with AI assistance

1. Read [Getting Started with aiRA](/aira-getting-started) to see what aiRA can do across Loyalty+, Engage+, and Insights+.
