Manage customer labels, statuses, registration, and subscription configurations from the self-serve Customers entity page.

The **Customers** entity page is where you manage customer configurations for your organization. It has four tabs: **Labels**, **Customer status**, **Registration**, and **Subscription**. The page header also includes quick links to **Member care** and **Audiences**.

To open the page, go to **Entities** > **Customers**.

## Labels

A **label** is a tag you attach to a customer to categorize or group them, for example, marking someone as a "VIP" customer or as having opted in to your newsletter. This is where you manage your organization's catalog of customer labels: create new labels, edit their details, and deactivate the ones you no longer use.

A label can also have an expiry configuration. After the configured duration or date, the label expires and is automatically removed from the customer.

## Use cases

Use customer labels to identify and group customers who share a trait:

- **VIP**: Identify your highest-spending customers who support and marketing teams can recognize them separately from your general customer base.
- **Newsletter Opt-in**: Identify customers who consent to marketing emails, to build an email campaign list.

## Creating a label

To create a label, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Entities** > **Customers**.

   The customer entities page opens.&#x20;

2. In the **Labels** tab, select **Create Label**.

   <Image src="https://files.readme.io/81f4f7ade0f246f734b88c89179595518987be700ede26adeab873f02a3fded9-Screenshot_2026-08-13_at_7.06.31_PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2026 08 13 at 7 06 31 PM" caption=" " />

3. Fill in the label details:

   | Field           | Description                                                                                                | Required |
   | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
   | **External ID** | A unique identifier from your own system, for example, a code you already use to track this tag elsewhere. | Yes      |
   | **Label Name**  | Name of the label, which is displayed across the product, for example, "VIP" or "Newsletter Opt-in."       | Yes      |
   | **Description** | Extra context about what the label means or when to use it.                                                | No       |

4. Set an expiry configuration in the **Assignment expiry** field  to control how long the label stays valid:

   | Option                         | Behavior                                                                      |
   | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | Never Expire                   | The label stays on the customer indefinitely.                                 |
   | Expire on a fixed date         | You choose a specific calendar date for it to expire.                         |
   | Expire after X days            | You enter a number of days; the label expires that many days after this save. |
   | Expire at end of current month | The label expires on the last day of the current month.                       |
   | Expire at end of current year  | The label expires on the last day of the current year.                        |
   | Expire at end of next year     | The label expires on the last day of next year.                               |

5. Select **Add Label**.

### Viewing and editing a label

To view or edit a label, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Entities** > **Customer** > **Labels**.
2. On the Labels list, view each label's **Name**, **Status**, **External ID**, **Expiry configuration**, and **Last updated** details. Select the **Name** column header to sort labels alphabetically. Use **Search by label name and IDs** to find a specific label, or the **Status** filter to show only **Active** or **Deactivated** labels.
3. Select a label to open its detail page. Review the label details, including the **Label ID**, **External ID**, **Name**, **Status**, **Description**, and **Expiry configuration**.
4. Select **Edit** to update the label details. You can edit the **External ID**, **Label name**, **Description**, and **Expiry configuration**. The **Label ID** cannot be edited. Select **Save Label**.

   The changes are saved for that label.  Use the **Status** filter above the list to switch between active and deactivated labels.

### Deactivating a label

To deactivate a label, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Entities** > **Customer** > **Labels**.
2. Locate the label you want to deactivate, select the **three-dot menu**, and then select **Deactivate**.
3. In the confirmation dialog box, select **Yes, deactivate**.

The label is deactivated and appears in the **Labels** listing with the **Deactivated** status.

A label can be activated or deactivated, but cannot be deleted.&#x20;

<Image src="https://files.readme.io/89f824aca4330f9441d9d176fad5c7063830c60bb062e13b596ea285b8e71d18-deactivate_label.gif" alt="Deactivate label" caption=" " />

### Activating a label

To activate a deactivated label, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Entities** > **Customer** > **Labels**.
2. On the **Labels** listing page, go to the **Status** filter and select **Deactivated**. This displays only the deactivated labels.
3. Locate the label you want to activate, select the **three-dot menu**, and then select **Activate**.
4. In the confirmation dialog box, select **Yes, activate**.

The label is activated and appears in the **Labels** listing with the **Activated** status.

## Customer status

The **Customer status** tab is where you turn on customer statuses for your organization and define status labels that match your terminology. Custom labels map to system-defined statuses, such as **Active** and **Suspended**, and you can assign a default status to customers at registration.

> **Note:** When you select **Enable customer status**, the **Fraud status** field stops working. Use customer statuses instead.

For how customer statuses work and the full configuration, see [Managing customer status](/customer_entity#managing-customer-lifecycle-with-customer-status).

## Registration and Subscription

Registration and subscription configurations currently live in your organization settings. Each tab links you to the right place:

1. Go to **Entities** > **Customers**.
2. Select the **Registration** or **Subscription** tab.
3. Select **Open in Org settings**. The configuration opens in a new browser tab.

> **Note:** These configurations are moving into their tabs in a later release. For now, manage them in your organization settings.

## Related modules

The **Customers** page header includes quick links to two related modules. Select a quick link to open that module in a new browser tab.

- **Member care**: Search for customer profiles and view individual customer details.
- **Audiences**: Create and manage the audience groups you use to target campaigns and journeys.
